# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a professional, respectful, and constructive project environment.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and professionally
- focus criticism on ideas and implementation, not people
- provide actionable technical feedback
- respect different backgrounds and experience levels
- prioritize patient safety, security, and engineering quality

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private or sensitive information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may moderate discussions, remove inappropriate content, or restrict participation when necessary to preserve a constructive environment.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
